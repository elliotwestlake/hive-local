## What's new

The panel is reorganised to work like a smart-heating app.

- **Tabs**: Overview, Rooms, Devices and Stats.
- **Heating and hot water pages**: tap a card on the Overview to open its page, with **Control** (− / + for the target, Schedule / Manual / Off, Boost), **Schedule** and **History** tabs.
- **Schedule**: pick a day from a row of day circles, then see its times ("06:30 – 08:30") with the temperature in a circle (a snowflake for off). Tap a time to change or remove it, **Add a time**, and the **⋯** button reads from the receiver or copies a day to weekdays, the weekend or every day. Changes wait on an **Unsaved changes** bar until you **Save**, and only the days you changed are written.
- **Stats**: heating history with **D / W / M**, time on or temperature, and arrows to step back through earlier periods. A summary at the top, then a bar per day (per hour for a day) with that day's lowest and highest room temperature. Hot water history is on the hot water page.
- **Rooms**: a **+** button adds a room.
- **Devices**: a card for each receiver (model, Zigbee2MQTT or ZHA, online, links to its pages) and each Hive radiator valve (room, battery, whether it's responding). The **+** adds a receiver or explains how to pair a valve.
