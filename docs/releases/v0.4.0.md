## What's new

- **Stats tab**: heating and hot water for Today, 7 days or 30 days: how long the boiler was on (vs the period before), how often it fired up, temperatures, boosts, a temperature and heating chart, time per day and time in each mode. Built from Home Assistant's history, so it needs the recorder (on by default); 30 days needs `purge_keep_days` raised above the default 10.
- **Header**: the tabs now sit beside the logo as pills, and wrap to their own row when space is tight.
