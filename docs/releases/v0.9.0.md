## New

- **Outside temperature in Stats** (#10). Pick a weather entity or an outdoor temperature sensor in the receiver's **Configure** (Settings › Devices & services › Hive Local). The history then shows each day's low and high outside (each hour's on the day view), next to the boiler's time on, and the note under the summary gives the average outside and whether it was colder or milder than the period before. It's read from Home Assistant's recorded history of that entity, converted to your unit system. Nothing changes until you pick one.
