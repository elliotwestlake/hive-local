## New

- **Outside temperature in Stats** (#10). Pick a weather entity or an outdoor temperature sensor in the new **Settings** tab. The history then shows each day's low and high outside (each hour's on the day view), next to the boiler's time on, and the note under the summary gives the average outside and whether it was colder or milder than the period before. It's read from Home Assistant's recorded history of that entity, converted to your unit system.
- **Settings tab.** The tabs are now **Overview · Devices · Stats · Settings**. Settings has the outside temperature, your rooms, and each receiver's boost defaults and frost protection (the same settings as its number entities).
- **Rooms moved into Settings**, and the Rooms tab is gone. Tapping a room on the Overview opens it in a pop-up (temperature, target, valves), and editing a room is a pop-up too.
- **Set up rooms from your areas:** one room per Home Assistant area that has Hive valves, with the area's temperature sensor; untick any you don't want.
- **Find a valve:** the bulb next to a valve makes it flash its light (when Home Assistant has an Identify button for it, as ZHA does).
- **Picking valves:** a scrolling list with the likely valves first, each with its area, reading, battery and room, and a search box when there are more than 8.
