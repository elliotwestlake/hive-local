## What's new

- **Pre-heat** (heating): starts heating early so the house is warm by the time the schedule says, instead of only starting to warm up then. Turn it on from the heating page's **Control** tab. It learns how fast your home warms up from Home Assistant's history (it needs 3 warm-ups first), and **Start at most** (default 2 h) caps how early it starts. It works by boosting to the new temperature until the scheduled time, so if Home Assistant stops, the receiver carries on with its schedule. Off by default. Closes #8.
- New entities: `switch.<receiver>_pre_heat` and `number.<receiver>_pre_heat_starts_at_most`.
- Stats counts a pre-heat as following the schedule, not as a boost.
