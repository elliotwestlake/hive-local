## What's new

- **Fewer, clearer tabs**: Overview, Rooms, Schedule and Stats.
- **Boost & mode is now on the Overview**: each card has Schedule / Manual / Off, a **Hold at** temperature when heating is on Manual, **Boost** with your defaults, and an arrow beside it to pick the boost length and temperature first.
- **One Schedule tab**: Heating and Hot water are now a switch at the top of the schedule editor, and switching keeps changes you haven't written yet. The **Schedule** chip on an Overview card opens that schedule.
- **Phones**: the tabs move to a bar along the bottom of the screen, and the Stats tiles sit two to a row.
