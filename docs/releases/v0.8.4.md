## Fixed

- **Schedule writes left junk slots on the thermostat.** A Hive receiver keeps 6 slots per day, and a day written with fewer left the rest as they were, often uninitialised: the SLT showed them as "68:15" at 15 °C. Every day is now written as 6 transitions, padding with repeats of the last one (as the receiver does itself), so it reads back unchanged.
- Reading a schedule ignores slots with an impossible time instead of showing "68:15" as 20:15.

Days already affected keep their junk slots until they're written again: open the Schedule tab, change and save each affected day (or change it on the thermostat).
