## What's new

A new Overview, laid out like a smart-heating app's home screen.

- **Quick actions**: boost heating or hot water with your defaults in one tap, or put everything back on its schedule.
- **Your devices**: filters (All, Heating, Hot water, Rooms) and a card for heating, hot water and each room, showing the temperature, what it's doing, and a **Boost** button. Low valve batteries show in amber.
- **Boost sheet**: Boost opens a sheet to pick the temperature and how long (30 min to 3 h), then boost. While boosting, the button reads **Boosting** and opens the time left and **Stop boost**.
- **Tap a card** for its controls: **−** / **+** for the heating target (like the thermostat: until the next scheduled change, the held temperature, or the boost temperature), Schedule / Manual / Off, and a link to its schedule. Tap a room to open it on the Rooms tab.
- **Look**: one orange accent, flat cards with no shadows, grey icons in shaded boxes, and cards that are always the same height.
